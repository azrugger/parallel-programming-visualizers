Speedup & Amdahl's Law

Six quick questions. Choose one answer for each, then select Check Answers to see your score and explanations. You can try again anytime.

1. A program takes 40 seconds with 1 core and 10 seconds with 4 cores. What is the speedup?
2. A program achieves a speedup of 6× using 8 cores. What is its parallel efficiency?
3. In Amdahl's Law, what does the quantity (1 − F) represent?
4. If F = 0.8, what speedup does Amdahl's Law predict using 4 cores (assuming no additional overhead)?
5. If 90% of a program can be parallelized (F = 0.9), what is its theoretical maximum speedup according to Amdahl's Law?
6. A program has a fixed parallel fraction F = 0.75. What happens to its Amdahl-predicted speedup as more and more cores are added?

Assume a fixed-size problem and ideal parallel execution except where otherwise noted. All questions are single-answer multiple choice.
